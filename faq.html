Senate Mashinani Sittings

Taking Parliament to the People — Article 126(1) of the Constitution empowers the Senate to hold sittings outside the Capital City of Nairobi to strengthen county oversight and public participation.

Senate Mashinani is an initiative by the Parliament of Kenya that relocates the entire Senate—including its plenary and committee sittings—from the traditional Parliament Buildings in Nairobi to one of the 47 counties for a week-long grassroots engagement.
